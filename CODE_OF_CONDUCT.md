# Code of Conduct — Community Guidelines 🤝

We want FinderX to be a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community. Everyone participating in this project (issues, PRs, discussions) is asked to follow these guidelines.

Be respectful

- Treat others with respect and kindness.
- Assume good intentions and give constructive feedback.
- Use inclusive language and avoid discriminatory remarks.

Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, harassment, or hateful language
- Discrimination based on gender, race, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or other protected characteristics
- Trolling, sustained disruptive behavior, or threats

Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. Open an issue titled `CODE OF CONDUCT - <brief title>` (or use a private channel if available).
2. Alternatively, contact the maintainers via the contact address listed in the repository settings.

Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, block users, or take other actions to keep the community safe. In serious cases, we may involve third parties.

Thank you for helping foster a positive community! 🙏
